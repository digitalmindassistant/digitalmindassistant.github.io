THE IDEA

Capture first. Organize with intention.

Digital Mind is being developed around a simple flow: make it easy to save a thought, then give it enough structure to become useful later.

01

Capture

Save a thought as a note, task, research topic, link, or reminder through the local application and supported personal capture channels.

02

Organize

Bring scattered ideas into one workspace where categories, search, and focused views make them easier to revisit.

03

Follow through

Turn selected tasks into reminders and user-requested calendar events while keeping the local record as the source of context.

PERSONAL, NOT PUBLIC

Built as a private workspace—not a social platform.

Digital Mind is currently a privately operated application in active development. It is not a hosted service and is not available for public accounts or signup.
